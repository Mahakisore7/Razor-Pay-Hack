# Code of Conduct

## Our standard

This project deals with other people's money and other people's attention. The
same care applies to how we treat each other.

Be direct about code and kind about people. Critique the design, not the designer.
Assume the person you disagree with has a reason you have not heard yet, and ask
for it before assuming they are wrong.

## Unacceptable behaviour

Harassment, personal attacks, discriminatory language, publishing others' private
information, and sustained disruption.

## Scope

Applies in this repository, its issues and pull requests, and any space where
someone is representing the project.

## Enforcement

Report concerns to the maintainer. Reports are handled confidentially. Maintainers
may edit, remove, or reject contributions that violate this document, and may ban
contributors for repeated or severe violations.
